Common questions

A few things you may want to know.

Who does STS work with?

STS primarily supports small businesses, professional practices, schools, churches, non-profits and other organisations across Trinidad & Tobago. Individual technology needs may also be considered.

Do you provide remote and on-site assistance?

Yes. The appropriate delivery method depends on the service, location, urgency and access required. This is clarified before work begins.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the agreed scope, complexity, required resources and deliverables. A quotation is provided before chargeable work begins.

Can STS guarantee that a business will not be hacked?

No responsible provider can guarantee complete security. STS helps clients understand risk, reduce avoidable weaknesses and improve preparedness through practical controls.

What should I include in an initial enquiry?

Briefly describe the problem, number of users or devices, location and desired outcome. Do not submit passwords, authentication codes, payment information or confidential client data.
