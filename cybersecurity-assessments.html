Assessment coverage

A structured review of people, process and technology.

The final scope is agreed before work begins. Depending on your needs, the assessment may cover the following areas.

01

Identity & access

User accounts, administrator privileges, password practices, MFA, onboarding and offboarding.

02

Email & cloud

Business email, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace settings, sharing practices and account recovery.

03

Devices & protection

Computers, updates, endpoint protection, encryption and avoidable configuration weaknesses.

04

Network security

Internet connectivity, Wi-Fi, routers, remote access and separation of trusted and guest devices.

05

Backups & resilience

Critical information, backup arrangements, restoration readiness and continuity considerations.

06

People & preparedness

Awareness, policies, incident contacts, reporting procedures and response readiness.

What you receive

Useful findings—not a document that sits on a shelf.

Deliverables are written so that business owners and decision-makers can understand the issues and take sensible action.

Executive summaryA concise view of the overall position.
Prioritised observationsIssues organised by importance and context.
Practical recommendationsRealistic actions based on your environment and budget.
Review discussionAn opportunity to clarify findings and next steps.

An assessment provides a point-in-time view based on the agreed scope and information available. It does not guarantee that every vulnerability will be identified or that a security incident cannot occur.
